The recovery kit is no longer something you can lose by tapping past it.

You can defer it, and the app keeps asking until one is saved. Confirming
now means typing two groups back from the kit you actually saved, so the
box cannot be ticked while holding nothing.

If you unlock with a fingerprint and forget your passphrase, you can now
set a new one with that fingerprint alone — no Recovery Kit needed.

New app icon. Print your kit straight to paper or a PDF.
